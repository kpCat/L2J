# Automatic curriculum — no hand-written millions of combinations

Use existing `PhantomTrainingScenarios`, `PhantomTrainingActors`, native SkillTree and Geo. Generate situations lazily from validated facts, not a giant Cartesian product and not 200 hand-authored test files. Deterministic seed and scene provenance. CPU budget and resource limits. Record rejected scenes with reason; do not count SKIP as training success.

- Feature dimensions: learned skill levels, classes, grade/enchant/SA/jewels where lawful, A/S gear now, actual buffs/debuffs, NPC type/level, 2D/3D range, current HP/MP, cast duration, **actual remaining CD/shared reuse**, role/Party size, target health, attack pressure, obstacles validated by Geo. Changing seed is NOT new independent observed context.
- At minimum native 004 corpus must cover Archmage damage in Solo and Party, healers Cardinal97 and EvaSaint105 (or explain specific lawful server inability without fake data), variation in level/gear/HP/MP/NPC and two genuinely distinct entities/situations per audit family. Do not force 61 archer to beat 68 mage or equalize class asymmetry. That is for future PvP, with true skill/equipment differences.
- Selection priorities: under-covered lawful classes/action families, novel real context, failures that are informative and safe to retry, historical scenarios for regression. Avoid endless retries of `NO_LEGAL_TARGET`, missing skills and bad Geo anchor. Adapt scene sampling based on measured accepted events/hour, not one-time changed ordinal seed.
- Train/test leakage: split by stable actor/spawn/episode/physical context, **not** per hit. Audit snapshot frozen; never tune on its outcome. When changing curriculum, new independent audit cohorts are required. Preserve prior used cohorts.
- Bounded sampler, example windows 4096 in existing network; long-term ledger and optional stratified replay on disk/SQL. No full data copies per tick. Keep configurable caps for storage and low-watermark disk; do not silently delete checkpoint or exact raw events. Offer export/prune with explicit retention policy.
- Native training examples count only after real receipt and COMMITTED transaction. Avoid fake victim kills, mass mobs, boss encounters, PvP winners or damage per hour derived from hit count.

Intended outcome: director automatically produces fresh valid episodes over consecutive sessions and maintains class/family coverage without Codex composing combinations. Benchmark generation overhead and actual useful ep/h. This is not promise of exploring 10 million scenes or proving 100% combat mastery.
